Methodology and source

Source: NYISO P-2A original CSV. Hourly day-ahead zonal LBMP, October 25, 2026. Retrieved October 26, 2026. External proxy locations excluded. This date has 24 hourly intervals; other dates require daylight-saving handling.

Mixed-integer optimization maximizes discharge receipts minus charging cost and wear. Charging efficiency and discharging efficiency each equal the square root of round-trip efficiency. Stored energy follows S[t] = S[t-1] + efficiency × charge − discharge / efficiency. Equivalent cycles are internal discharged energy divided by storage capacity. The displayed scenarios are precomputed by the included Python model, not solved by an AI model.
